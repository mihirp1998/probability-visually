Probability, visually

Interactive notes on probability, statistics and puzzles for quant and ML interviews. Every idea comes with the intuition first, the formula second, and a widget you can play with until it clicks.

How to use these notes

  • Read the yellow 💡 boxes first. They give the one-sentence intuition you should be able to say out loud in an interview.
  • Play with every ▶ widget. Drag the sliders and predict what will happen before you look.
  • Proofs are folded away under ▸ toggles. Open them on a second pass.
  • 🧠 Quick checks test whether the idea actually stuck.
  • Purple ✏️ boxes mark places where the original notes had a typo or error that has been corrected.

The five moves that solve most problems

1 · Linearity
$\E[\sum X_i]=\sum\E[X_i]$, even when the $X_i$ are dependent.
2 · Indicators
Count = sum of 0/1 variables; $\E[\1_A]=P(A)$.
3 · First step
Condition on the first move, then solve for the unknown.
4 · Complement
$P(\text{at least one})=1-P(\text{none})$.
5 · Symmetry
Equivalent things get equal probability, so divide evenly.
